ngoIntro: |
  I've written a lot lately about how the field of alignment deprioritized the kind of research that
  aims at a deep and fundamental understanding of intelligence, in favor of pragmatic engineering work.
  This curriculum is intended to help people orient themselves to the former. Much of it is inspired by
  MIRI's original agent foundations research, but I've included several other important threads
  (including active inference, LLM naturalism, and the study of anti-normativity).

  This curriculum is very skewed towards the ideas I personally find important (which is also why
  there's so much of my own work, since those are the ideas I write about). However, I'm open to
  including other readings on ideas I'm less familiar with, as long as they focus on deep conceptual
  understanding; suggestions (and other comments on this doc) welcome.
